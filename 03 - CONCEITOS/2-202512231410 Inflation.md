# CONCEITO

Inflation it self is the phenomena of a certain quantity of money don't buy the same thing as it use to. But we can distinguish two forms of inflation: monetary expansion and price inflation. 

Monetary expansion is simply the increase of the money supply, what is very intuitive, if we have more money available, it will debase. The hole problem the book brings us is that, in the actual monetary system,  governments and private banks have the power to control the increase of the money supply by: printing more money, loans, creating debt  ... . What leads to a hot take: monetary expansion is a hidden tax.

Price inflation, that one that is showned on tv is the mean of price rises.
